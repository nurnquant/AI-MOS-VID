# I008 — They Saw You Skip Salah

Series: B — The Hard Parts
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child saw you miss salah. They said nothing. They noticed everything.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> This is not a disaster. It is the most useful moment you will get all year.

## Beats

- Hiding it teaches that faith is a performance.
- Naming it teaches that faith has repair.
- "I missed Asr. I am praying it now" — that is the entire lesson.
- Children forgive honest adults far faster than adults expect.

## Close

> They do not need a perfect parent. They need one who goes back.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the hard ones.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Taboo topic, which is precisely why it travels.
- Relief-based rather than shame-based, consistent with the brand's voice.

## Risks and honest limits

- Could be read as normalising missed prayers. The close must be repair, never permission.
- Recommend a scholarly read.
