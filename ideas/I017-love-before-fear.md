# I017 — Love Before Fear

Series: D — Things Nobody Told You
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> A four-year-old who is frightened of Allah is not ahead. They are hurt.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Mercy comes first. It is in the first line of the Book.

## Beats

- Bismillah names mercy twice before anything else is said.
- Children under seven cannot hold abstract fear without it becoming anxiety.
- Ar-Rahman before anything else.
- There is time for the whole picture. It is not age four.

## Close

> Start with the Lord who loves them. Everything else has somewhere to sit after that.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- High emotional charge and a real corrective — many parents in this audience were raised the other way and are deciding right now whether to repeat it.

## Risks and honest limits

- HIGHEST RISK OF ALL THIRTY. Touches aqeedah and pedagogy at once, and some will read it as softening the deen.
- Do not produce without a scholarly read. Route the exact script past a qualified teacher on your team first.
- If that sign-off is not available, drop this one rather than soften it into mush.
