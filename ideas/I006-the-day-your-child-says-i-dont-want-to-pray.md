# I006 — The Day Your Child Says I Don't Want to Pray

Series: B — The Hard Parts
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> One day your child will say they do not want to pray. Here is what not to do.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Do not make salah the fight. Make it the thing that happens.

## Beats

- Force produces compliance that expires at eighteen.
- Ask why once, then actually listen. It is usually tired or bored, not rebellion.
- Pray next to them without commentary.
- Let them see you pray when nobody made you.

## Close

> They are not rejecting Allah. They are testing whether you will stay calm.

## Follow-ask

> "The Hard Parts" — follow, we are doing the ones nobody posts about.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- The single most common private worry in this audience, and almost nobody makes calm content about it.
- Comment volume will be large, and comment volume is what Reels rewards.

## Risks and honest limits

- HIGH. Any hint of "so don't make them pray" draws heavy criticism and deserves it.
- The script must hold the line explicitly: the obligation is not in question, the method is.
- Worth a scholarly read before publishing.
