# I012 — The Car Is a Masjid

Series: C — Two Minutes
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You spend hours a week in the car with your child. More than most weekend Islamic schools.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You do not need a lesson plan. You need the radio off for one journey.

## Beats

- One short surah on repeat for a week beats a different one each day.
- They learn it without being taught.
- Ask what they think it is about, and accept any answer.
- The car has no eye contact, which is exactly why children say more in it.

## Close

> Same drive. Same minutes. Different week.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest of "Two Minutes".

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Reframes dead time as an asset — a reframe, not an instruction, so it costs the parent nothing to accept.
- The no-eye-contact observation is the part people quote.

## Risks and honest limits

- Do NOT invent a statistic. Either verify a real average for family car time or keep it vague as written. A fabricated number is the fastest way to lose a knowledgeable audience.
