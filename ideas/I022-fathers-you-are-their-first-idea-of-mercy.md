# I022 — Fathers: You Are Their First Idea of Mercy

Series: E — You Are Not Failing
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child will meet mercy through you long before they meet it in a book.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> That is not pressure. It is the job description, and it is smaller than you think.

## Beats

- A father who listens teaches that being heard is possible.
- Being gentle while you are the biggest person in the room is the lesson.
- Showing up on an ordinary Tuesday outranks the grand gesture.
- They build their expectations of authority from the parent who was there.

## Close

> You do not have to be impressive. You have to be safe.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Fathers are under-served by this entire content category. Low competition, and wives share it to husbands, which doubles the reach per view.

## Risks and honest limits

- THEOLOGICAL FRAMING NEEDS CARE. Say "their first human experience of mercy" — never anything that reads as a comparison between a parent and Allah.
- Flag for a scholarly read before recording.
