# I024 — The Parent Who Reverted

Series: E — You Are Not Failing
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You came to Islam at twenty-six. Your child starts at four. You feel behind.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You are handing them the thing you had to go and find.

## Beats

- No Arabic, no family memory to lean on, and still the house is Muslim because you made it so.
- Children of reverts often hold it more deliberately, because they watched it being chosen.
- You are allowed to learn beside them, out loud.
- Find one teacher so you are not the only source in the house.

## Close

> You did not inherit this. You chose it. Your child gets both.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Reverts are a large, loyal and chronically under-addressed audience. Near-zero competition on this exact framing.
- The honest bridge to a teacher is built into the argument rather than bolted on.

## Risks and honest limits

- Handle family situations gently. Many reverts have painful ones and the script must not dramatise them for effect.
