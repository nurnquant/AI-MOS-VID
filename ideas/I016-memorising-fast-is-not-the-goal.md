# I016 — Memorising Fast Is Not the Goal

Series: D — Things Nobody Told You
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child memorised a surah in two days. Now ask them what it means.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Hifz without meaning holds until puberty and then leaks.

## Beats

- Speed gets celebrated because it is the only thing that is easy to measure.
- Meaning is what makes a teenager keep it.
- Ask for one word they understood per page.
- Slower with meaning beats faster without. Every time.

## Close

> You are not raising a recording. You are raising a reader.

## Follow-ask

> "Things Nobody Told You" — five parts. Follow.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Contradicts prevailing community pressure, which is exactly why it gets shared and argued about. Argument is reach.

## Risks and honest limits

- Will draw disagreement from hifz-first families. Defensible and worth saying — but be respectful and never imply hifz programmes are wrong.
