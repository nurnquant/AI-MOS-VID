# I009 — Your Child Lied to You

Series: B — The Hard Parts
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child looked straight at you and lied. Do not start with the punishment.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Start by working out what they were afraid of.

## Beats

- Most young lying is fear management, not corruption.
- If the punishment for lying is bigger than the punishment for the thing lied about, you have taught them to lie better.
- Tell them the truth is always survivable in this house.
- Then keep that promise the first time it is inconvenient.

## Close

> A child who tells you the hard truth at seven will tell you the hard truth at seventeen.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the hard ones.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- The punishment-asymmetry line is a genuine insight and the kind of thing that gets screenshotted.

## Risks and honest limits

- Discipline content draws strong disagreement in comments. Acceptable — but do not moralise, and do not imply lying should go unaddressed.
