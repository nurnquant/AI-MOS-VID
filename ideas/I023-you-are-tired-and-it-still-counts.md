# I023 — You Are Tired and It Still Counts

Series: E — You Are Not Failing
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You made dua half asleep and assumed it did not count.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> The tired one counts.

## Beats

- Worship that costs effort is not the lesser kind.
- Exhausted dhikr while rocking a baby is worship that the childless never get the chance to offer.
- These years of small, broken worship are a season, not your permanent record.
- Ask for ease, not for more energy.

## Close

> This season is not a gap in your deen. It is your deen right now.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Speaks to parents of under-fives — the exact group that becomes a trial booking eighteen months later.
- Enormous share potential inside family group chats.

## Risks and honest limits

- Keep reward claims modest. Do not assert relative reward levels, and do not quote a hadith unless it is verified with a source on screen.
- This is the one where an unsourced quote would do real damage to credibility.
