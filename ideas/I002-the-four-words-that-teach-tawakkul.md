# I002 — The Four Words That Teach Tawakkul

Series: A — You're Already Teaching Them
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child learns tawakkul from four words. You already say three of them.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Bismillah. Alhamdulillah. InshaAllah. Astaghfirullah. That is the whole syllabus for a five-year-old.

## Beats

- Bismillah — I am not starting this alone.
- Alhamdulillah — good things came from somewhere.
- InshaAllah — I do not control tomorrow.
- Astaghfirullah — adults get things wrong and say so out loud.

## Close

> Four words, said where they can hear you. No curriculum needed.

## Follow-ask

> Episode 2 of 5.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Highly saveable. List formats get bookmarked, and saves weigh heavily on Reels.
- The differentiator is translating each word into what the CHILD learns, not what the word means. The meanings are everywhere; this framing is not.

## Risks and honest limits

- "Four words" list formats are common. Without the child-side translation this is generic and will underperform.
