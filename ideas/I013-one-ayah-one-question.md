# I013 — One Ayah, One Question

Series: C — Two Minutes
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Dinner table. One ayah. One question. That is the whole thing.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Not a lecture. A question you do not already know the answer to.

## Beats

- Read one short ayah in translation.
- Ask what they think it means and do not correct the first answer.
- Ask what it would look like in this house this week.
- Stop there. Even if it is going well.

## Close

> Stopping while they still want more is why they come back tomorrow.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Gives a repeatable ritual with an explicit stop rule. The stop rule is the unusual, memorable part.

## Risks and honest limits

- Frame as reflection, never as tafsir. Keep untrained adults out of exegesis — say so in the script if needed.

## Before production

- You approve the idea.
- You name the finishing style — see [library/STYLES.md](../library/STYLES.md).
- You choose the voice: an AI read, or your own. Parent-facing content converts
