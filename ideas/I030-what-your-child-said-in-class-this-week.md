# I030 — What Your Child Said in Class This Week

Series: F — Inside the Class
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> A six-year-old asked our teacher whether Allah gets tired. Here is what happened next.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> The teacher did not correct her. She thanked her.

## Beats

- A real question from a real class, anonymised.
- The teacher's answer — short, and at the child's size.
- Why the question was welcomed instead of shut down.
- What the child did the following week.

## Close

> The best sign a child is learning is not a perfect recitation. It is a strange question.

## Follow-ask

> Follow — a new one every week, as long as parents keep saying yes.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Endlessly repeatable format, which is the most followable structure in the whole set.
- Shows the teaching relationship instead of describing it. Nothing else in the series can do that.

## Risks and honest limits

- CHILD PRIVACY IS A HARD GATE, NOT A PREFERENCE. No name, no face, no voice, no recognisable detail.
- Written parent permission for every single episode, and permission for this specific use, not a general one.
- If permission is not clean and current, the episode does not run. There is no version of this worth a child's privacy.
- See docs/security/AIVS-media-security-baseline.md — the same standard applies to published anecdotes as to media.
