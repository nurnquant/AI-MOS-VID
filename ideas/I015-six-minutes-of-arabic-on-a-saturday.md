# I015 — Six Minutes of Arabic on a Saturday

Series: C — Two Minutes
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Six minutes on a Saturday beats an hour once a month. It is not close.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Spacing beats volume. That is not motivation advice, it is how memory works.

## Beats

- Same time, same place, six minutes.
- Sounds and letters only. No writing at first.
- Finish before they are bored. Always.
- A month of this is twenty-four minutes, and they will know more than after a term of cramming.

## Close

> You are not trying to teach Arabic. You are trying to make it ordinary.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- The counterintuitive arithmetic is the hook.
- Strong, honest bridge to structured classes — the video argues for consistency, and classes are how consistency happens.

## Risks and honest limits
