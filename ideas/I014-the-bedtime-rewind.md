# I014 — The Bedtime Rewind

Series: C — Two Minutes
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Three good things and one dua. Ninety seconds. Do it in the dark.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> Gratitude is a muscle, and bedtime is when a child is honest.

## Beats

- Ask for three things from today that Allah gave them.
- Accept "chips". Small counts.
- Then one dua, together, same words every night.
- Never use it to review their behaviour, or it becomes an interrogation.

## Close

> They will start doing the three things unprompted. That is shukr becoming a habit.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Pairs directly with the short-bedtime-dua idea. Cross-referenced episodes build a body of work, which is what makes a page worth following rather than a video worth watching.

## Risks and honest limits

- Low.
