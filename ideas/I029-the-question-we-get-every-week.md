# I029 — The Question We Get Every Week

Series: F — Inside the Class
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Every week a parent asks the same thing: is my child too young to start?

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> The answer depends on one thing, and it is not age.

## Beats

- Can they sit for fifteen minutes with something they chose?
- If yes, they are ready, even at four.
- If no, wait. Six months is an enormous difference at that age.
- Starting too early and quitting is worse than starting later.

## Close

> We would rather tell you to wait than take a booking that fails.

## Follow-ask

> Follow — we answer one of these a week.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- The close is the whole asset. Turning away business is the most trust-building thing a service can say, and it converts better than any offer.
- Repeatable weekly format, so it builds a following habit rather than a single spike.

## Risks and honest limits

- Only say it if you mean it. If a parent then says they will wait, you must actually support that and follow up later. Saying this and then pushing the sale is worse than never saying it.
