# I018 — Stop Correcting Their Tajweed at Home

Series: D — Things Nobody Told You
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Every time you correct them mid-ayah at home, they read a little less.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You are their audience, not their second teacher.

## Beats

- Correction from a parent lands differently than from a teacher. It costs safety.
- Let them finish, then praise one specific thing.
- Save corrections for the person trained to give them.
- A child who reads imperfectly and often beats one who reads perfectly and rarely.

## Close

> Home is where they practise. Class is where they are corrected.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Specific, actionable, and contradicts what the most motivated parents are doing right now. That is the mark of a real "nobody told you".

## Risks and honest limits

- Could read as discouraging parental involvement. The close fixes it — home has a role, just a different one. Do not cut the close for length.
