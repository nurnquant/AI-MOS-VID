# I027 — Why We Teach the Sound Before the Letter

Series: F — Inside the Class
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Most children are taught the letter's name first. We do it the other way round.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> A child who can say "alif, baa, taa" still cannot read a single word.

## Beats

- Names are labels. Sounds are the machine.
- Sounds let them blend on day one.
- Blending on day one is why they do not quit in week three.
- The names come later, and stick better, once the sounds mean something.

## Close

> The goal is not naming letters. It is reading a word by themselves, early.
