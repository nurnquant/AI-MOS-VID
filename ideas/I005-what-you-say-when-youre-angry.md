# I005 — What You Say When You're Angry

Series: A — You're Already Teaching Them
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child will not repeat what you taught them. They will repeat what you said when you lost your temper.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> That is the lesson that actually transferred.

## Beats

- They memorise the tone before the words.
- Astaghfirullah, out loud, after you snap — that is a complete tarbiyah lesson.
- Apologising to a child does not weaken you.
- They learn that repentance is possible by watching you do it.

## Close

> You do not need to never lose your temper. You need them to see what you do next.

## Follow-ask

> Episode 5 of 5. Then start again at episode 1.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Highest emotional charge in the series.
- "Apologise to your child" is one of the most-shared ideas in this whole category.
- The close forgives the viewer, which is why they follow instead of feeling scolded.

## Risks and honest limits

- Strongest guilt risk of the five. The close must arrive fast — if the beats run long it wounds instead of freeing.
