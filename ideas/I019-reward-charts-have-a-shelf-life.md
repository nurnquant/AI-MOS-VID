# I019 — Reward Charts Have a Shelf Life

Series: D — Things Nobody Told You
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> The sticker chart worked. That is the problem.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You paid them to pray. The price only goes up from here.

## Beats

- External rewards work fast, then need topping up.
- Children stop when the reward stops. That is the definition of the wrong habit.
- Swap the reward for company — pray with them instead of paying them.
- Name the feeling afterwards, not the prize.

## Close

> You want the habit to survive the day you stop watching.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Almost every parent in this audience has a chart on the fridge. Personal, immediate, slightly uncomfortable — and therefore memorable.

## Risks and honest limits

- Do not condemn parents using charts. The honest version is that charts can start something and cannot finish it.
