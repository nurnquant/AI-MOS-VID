# I025 — When the Grandparents Disagree

Series: E — You Are Not Failing
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your parents think you are too strict. Or not strict enough. Both hurt.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You do not have to win the argument to run your own house.

## Beats

- Culture and deen get tangled, and separating them politely is most of the work.
- Disagree in private. Never contradict each other in front of the child.
- Give grandparents a role they can own — a story, a dua, a memory.
- The child does not need the adults to be identical. They need them not to be at war.

## Close

> Your child can hold two houses. What they cannot hold is being the battlefield.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Intensely relatable and almost never addressed. Generates long comment threads, which Reels rewards more than watch time alone.

## Risks and honest limits

- Could read as licensing disrespect towards parents. The script must explicitly protect the grandparents' status while keeping the parents' authority. That balance is the whole craft of this one.
