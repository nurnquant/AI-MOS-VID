# I026 — What One-on-One Actually Looks Like

Series: F — Inside the Class
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You booked a trial class and have no idea what happens in it. Here is the whole thing.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> No slides, no group of twenty, no camera on your child unless you want one.

## Beats

- Teacher greets them. Three minutes of review.
- New material, then one dua, then done.
- Thirty minutes, because that is a child's attention span.
- You can sit in. Most parents do for the first two.

## Close

> That is it. That is the whole class.

## Follow-ask

> Follow to see how the weeks build.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Removes the friction that actually blocks bookings. It is rarely price — it is not knowing what you are walking into.
- Lowest reach in the set, highest conversion.

## Risks and honest limits

- This is an advertisement. Keep the tone honest about that. Do not dress a sales video as a parenting insight — that trade destroys the trust the other twenty-nine build.

## Before production

- You approve the idea.
- You name the finishing style — see [library/STYLES.md](../library/STYLES.md).
- You choose the voice: an AI read, or your own. Parent-facing content converts
  noticeably better in a real human voice.
- You choose on-camera or narrated over scenes.
- Generation needs the Higgsfield connector authorised.

Rough cost once approved: about 90 credits for a 30 s three-clip build. Everything
after generation is local and free.
