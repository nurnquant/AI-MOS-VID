# I021 — The Mother Who Thinks She Is Failing

Series: E — You Are Not Failing
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You did not do Qur'an time today. Or yesterday. You are still not failing.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You measured a whole year by two days.

## Beats

- The days you fed them, held them and kept the house standing were not neutral. That was tarbiyah.
- Consistency is measured in months, not evenings.
- The standard you are holding yourself to was invented on the internet.
- Name one thing you did do this week.

## Close

> A tired mother who keeps showing up is the whole plan. There is no better version of this.

## Follow-ask

> "You Are Not Failing" — follow if you needed to hear that.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Highest share potential in the entire set.
- Direct address, second person, zero instructions. Nothing to do means nothing to resist.
- The follow-ask matches the emotional state instead of interrupting it.

## Risks and honest limits

- Do not attach a hard CTA. A sales pitch on this one would sour it completely — keep the free-trial line minimal, or drop it entirely and take the reach.
