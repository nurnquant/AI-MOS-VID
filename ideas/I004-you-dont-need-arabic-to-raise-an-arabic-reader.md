# I004 — You Don't Need Arabic to Raise an Arabic Reader

Series: A — You're Already Teaching Them
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You cannot read Arabic. You think that is your child's ceiling.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> It is not. You are not the teacher. You are the reason they show up.

## Beats

- Your job is the appointment kept, not the tajweed.
- Children who see a parent struggling with the letters try harder, not less.
- Learning beside them beats testing them.
- Every reverted parent and every non-Arab parent has had this exact fear.

## Close

> Your child can go further in Arabic than you ever will. That is not a failure. That is the point.

## Follow-ask

> Episode 4 of 5.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Names non-Arab and reverted parents directly — a large, loyal, under-addressed slice.
- Natural bridge to a trial class without a hard sell: the video's own logic is "get one teacher".

## Risks and honest limits

- Closest thing to a sales message in this series. Keep the CTA soft or it reads as an ad and the trust drains.
