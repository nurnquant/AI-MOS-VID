# I020 — They Copy Your Phone, Not Your Lecture

Series: D — Things Nobody Told You
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You talked to them about deen for ten minutes. They watched you scroll for two hours.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> The lecture is not the input. The evidence is.

## Beats

- A child measures what matters by where the hours go.
- One visible habit beats ten instructions.
- Put the phone face-down for salah where they can see you do it.
- You do not have to be off your phone. You have to be seen choosing.

## Close

> They are not listening to you. They are auditing you.

## Follow-ask

> Follow for the rest.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- "They are auditing you" is the screenshot line.
- Universal self-recognition — and it is being watched on a phone, which does the work for you.

## Risks and honest limits

- Guilt-heavy. Needs the forgiving beat ("seen choosing") or it wounds. Do not cut that beat for length.
