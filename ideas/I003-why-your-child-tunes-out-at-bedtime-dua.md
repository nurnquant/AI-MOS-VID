# I003 — Why Your Child Tunes Out at Bedtime Dua

Series: A — You're Already Teaching Them
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~30 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> Your child goes limp during bedtime dua. It is not disrespect.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> It is length. You are running an adult's routine at a child's bedtime.

## Beats

- Ninety seconds is a wall for a four-year-old.
- One dua. Same words. Every night.
- Same words matter more than more words — repetition is what they can own.
- If they can finish it without you, it is theirs.

## Close

> Short and every night beats long and sometimes. Every time.

## Follow-ask

> Episode 3 of 5.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. This line is the
mechanism — without it the Reel earns reach and no followers.

## Why this should convert

- Names a behaviour parents privately blame themselves for.
- The fix is subtraction, not addition. Almost no parenting content tells you to do less, which is exactly why it is memorable.

## Risks and honest limits

- Could read as lowering the standard. The close has to land on consistency or the point inverts.
