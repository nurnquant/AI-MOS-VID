# I001 — You're Already Teaching Them

Series: A — You're Already Teaching Them
Status: proposed — nothing built, nothing spent
Audience: parents. Not children.
Format: ~22 s vertical, muted-autoplay safe
Style: not yet named — you name it, it is never guessed

## Hook

> You think you're not teaching your child deen.

First 1.5 s. On-screen text carries this alone, because Facebook autoplays muted
and a hook that only exists in the audio does not exist.

## The turn

> You are. You just don't count it.

## Beats

- Bismillah before the car. They heard it.
- Alhamdulillah when you were exhausted.
- You apologised when you were wrong.
- Dua out loud when the news was bad.

## Close

> They learn deen the way they learned to talk. From you.

## Follow-ask

On-screen text over the brand tag. **Not spoken.**

> Episode 2: the four words that teach tawakkul.
> You already say three of them.
> Follow so you catch it.

Follows come from a series promise, not from one good video. Two changes from
the first draft, both deliberate:

**Naming the next episode beats promising a series.** "Follow for more" asks for
trust. A specific, curious claim about tomorrow gives a reason.

**The original ask was "the next four are the ones you're getting wrong."** It
would have worked — guilt drives follows — but it reintroduced in the last line
exactly what the previous twenty seconds removed, and the anti-guilt position is
the only thing here that competitors are not already doing. Not worth trading
away for one line.

Keeping it out of the voiceover buys back about five seconds and puts the ask
where a muted viewer will actually read it.

## Timing

**52 spoken words.** At a conversational 2.6 a second that is about 20 s of
voice, and the pauses between beats take it to roughly 22 s with the tag. The
word count is what sets the length — if the read comes back long, cut words,
do not speed up the delivery.

| Time          | What                                    |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 0.0 - 2.5 s   | Hook. Text and voice together.          |
| 2.5 - 5.0 s   | The turn.                               |
| 5.0 - 15.5 s  | Four beats, about 2.6 s each.           |
| 15.5 - 20.0 s | Close.                                  |
| 20.0 - 22.0 s | Brand tag, with the follow-ask as text. |

Cut from ~30 s. A first-time viewer with no face on screen will not hold for
thirty seconds, and every beat here earns its place at twenty-two.

**"They heard it" survives on beat one only.** Repeating it on every beat was
the stronger device and it cost three seconds; carrying it once sets the pattern
and lets the ear supply it for the rest. If the piece comes in short at the edit,
that repetition is the first thing to put back.

## Why this should convert

- Anti-guilt is rare in this niche. Every competing page runs on "are you doing enough?" — that gets views and burns goodwill.
- Relief gets shared to a spouse. Guilt gets scrolled past.
- The series promise is the follow mechanism, not the content.

## Risks and honest limits

- One Reel will not move follower count. Five in ten days might.
- Anti-guilt caps the ceiling slightly — outrage travels further. Not recommending outrage.
