## 5.1 Smile and Say Salam (`5.1-smile-and-say-salam`)

The Prophet ﷺ greeted children first. So can yours. 🤝

He would pass by children playing and give them salam before they gave it to him.
The Messenger of Allah, greeting kids first.

Teach your child that salam is not a formality reserved for adults — it is a
small gift of peace they can hand to anyone, any time.

## 5.2 Help Your Parents (`5.2-help-your-parents`)

Helping at home is not a chore — it is worship with a smile. 🍽️

Setting a plate. Folding a towel. Carrying something heavier than they should.
Small hands helping willingly is birr al-walidayn in its earliest form.

Thank them out loud when they do it. Children repeat whatever gets noticed.

👇 What is your child's favourite way to help around the house?

#HelpYourParents #BirrAlWalidayn #KindKids #HomeHelpers #IslamicManners #LittleMuslims #RiwaqAlIlm

## 5.3 Share Your Toys (`5.3-share-your-toys`)

Sharing is the first sadaqah a child ever gives. 🧸

Long before they can give money, they can give up the toy they were holding. It
costs them something real — and that is exactly what makes it count.

Do not force it. Praise it loudly when it happens on its own.

👇 Is sharing easy or hard for your little one right now?

#Sharing #FirstSadaqah #GenerousKids #Akhlaq #LittleMuslims #KindnessMatters #RiwaqAlIlm

## 5.4 Tell the Truth (`5.4-tell-the-truth`)

Truth is easier to say when a child knows they will still be loved. 🤍

Children lie mostly out of fear. If honesty is met with calm instead of anger, a
child learns that telling the truth is safe — and that lesson holds long after
the broken cup is forgotten.

The Prophet ﷺ said truthfulness leads to righteousness. It starts with small
confessions being received gently.

👇 How do you respond when your child owns up to something?

#Honesty #Sidq #TruthfulKids #IslamicValues #GentleParenting #Akhlaq #RiwaqAlIlm

## 5.5 Be Thankful (`5.5-be-thankful`)

Gratitude is a habit before it is a feeling. 🍎

Small children do not naturally feel thankful — they feel hungry, tired, excited.
Gratitude is trained: say alhamdulillah out loud, name the blessing, repeat it
daily. The feeling follows the habit, not the other way round.

👇 What does your family say alhamdulillah for most often?

#Shukr #Gratitude #Alhamdulillah #ThankfulKids #LittleMuslims #IslamicValues #RiwaqAlIlm

## 5.6 Help a Friend (`5.6-help-a-friend`)

The best of people are the most useful to others. 📚

Picking up dropped books. Sitting with the child who is alone. Explaining the bit
the friend missed. None of it looks like worship, and all of it is.

Teach your child that being useful is a form of good character, not a favour they
are owed something for.

👇 Tell us about a time your child helped someone without being asked. ❤️

#HelpAFriend #Ukhuwah #KindKids #BestOfPeople #SchoolKindness #Akhlaq #RiwaqAlIlm

## 5.7 Spread Kindness (`5.7-spread-kindness`)

Kindness costs a child nothing and teaches them everything. 🌸

A flower handed to a neighbour. A smile at the checkout. Holding a door. The
Prophet ﷺ described faith itself as being adorned with good character — and
children learn character in these tiny, unremarkable moments.
