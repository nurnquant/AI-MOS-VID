## 1.5 Wudu Together (`1.5-wudu-together`)

## Posted on FB and INsta , TT

Before the prayer comes the water — and the lesson. 💧

He is too small to understand every rule yet. But he understands that his father
stops, rolls up his sleeves, and does this carefully every single day. That is
the lesson landing, long before the fiqh does.

Teach the order. Let them copy the culture. Wudu is the first act of worship most
children ever perform on their own — make it feel gentle, not rushed.

## 1.6 When the Adhan Plays (`1.6-when-the-adhan-plays`)

The moment the adhan starts, the whole house changes. 🕌

Toys go quiet. Heads lift. Nobody had to say a word — because in this home, that
sound has always meant something.

Children learn reverence from atmosphere, not instruction. If the adhan
interrupts us, it will interrupt them. If we ignore it, they learn that too.

## 1.7 The Quran Shelf (`1.7-the-quran-shelf`)

Where the Quran sits in your home tells your children what matters. 📖

Not locked away on the highest shelf, untouched. Not buried under paperwork
either. Somewhere they can see it, reach toward it, and be taught how to hold it.

A child who grows up watching the Quran treated with honour will treat it that
way for life — without ever being lectured about it.
