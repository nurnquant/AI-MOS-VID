## 2.4 Learning Tajweed (`2.4-learning-tajweed`)

Tajweed is not decoration — it is how the words were meant to sound. 🎧

A letter said from the wrong part of the mouth becomes a different letter. A
meaning can shift entirely. This is why recitation is taught mouth-to-ear, with
a teacher listening, and not from an app alone.

Children pick up correct makhraj far faster than adults. The window is now.

## 2.6 Reading Together (`2.6-reading-together`)

A child reading by themselves is doing homework. A child reading with a parent's
finger tracking the line beside them is making a memory — and memories are what
they come back to as adults.

Ten minutes on the same page beats an hour of supervision from across the room.
