## 4.1 Morning Dua (`4.1-morning-dua`)

The first sentence of her day set the tone for all of it. 🌅

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

_Alhamdulillahilladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur_

"All praise to Allah who gave us life after death, and to Him is the return."

Sleep is a small death, and waking is a small resurrection. A child who learns to
say this on waking learns that every single morning is a gift that was given
back to them.

## 4.2 Dua Before Eating (`4.2-dua-before-eating`)

One word turns a meal into worship. 🌴

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

_Bismillah_ — "In the name of Allah."

And if they forget and remember mid-meal:
_Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu_ — "In the name of Allah, at its beginning and
its end."

It takes a second. It teaches a lifetime of noticing where food actually comes
from.

## 4.3 Dua Before Sleeping (`4.3-dua-before-sleeping`)

Teach her this, and she will never sleep unprotected. 🌙

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

_Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya_

"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."

Six words. Said in the dark, half-asleep, night after night — until one day it
comes out of them automatically, and they carry that comfort into adulthood.

## 4.4 Dua for Parents (`4.4-dua-for-parents`)

The dua every parent hopes to hear one day. 🤲❤️

رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

_Rabbi irhamhuma kama rabbayani sagheera_

"My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small."
— Quran 17:24

We spend years raising them, praying over them, carrying them. Then one night
they raise their small hands and pray for us.

Teach your child this dua and you have given yourself a gift that outlives you.

👇 Say "Ameen" if you want your children to make this dua for you.

## 4.5 Dua for Knowledge (`4.5-dua-for-knowledge`)

Before she opens her notebook, she opens her heart. 📚

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

_Rabbi zidni ilma_

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge." — Quran 20:114

Four words, taught to the Prophet ﷺ himself. A child who says this before
studying learns that knowledge is not just earned — it is asked for, and granted.

## 4.6 Dua for Guidance (`4.6-dua-for-guidance`)

When a child does not know which way to go, teach them who to ask. 🕯️

اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ

_Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqeem_

"Guide us to the straight path." — Quran 1:6

We already say it seventeen times a day inside Al-Fatihah. Pull it out, explain
it once, and your child will never recite that verse absentmindedly again.

## 4.7 Dua When Leaving Home (`4.7-dua-leaving-home`)

Three sentences at the door, and she leaves protected. 🚪

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

_Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah_

"In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no power except
with Allah."

School runs are rushed. Shoes go missing. But the ten seconds at the door are
the ten seconds your child will remember when they are grown and leaving their
own house.
