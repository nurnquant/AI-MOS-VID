**Text to Video — Veo**

One continuous cinematic take inside a beautiful traditional Islamic learning hall inspired by the historic riwaqs of Al-Azhar.

A dignified Muslim Qur'an teacher in a simple cream thobe slowly walks through the quiet hall carrying a large antique handcrafted mirror.

Beside him walks a young Muslim boy holding a closed Qur'an respectfully against his chest.

The camera remains in a wide shot and slowly tracks alongside them.

The physical room is peaceful and understated: elegant Islamic arches, warm stone walls, wooden bookshelves filled with classical books, patterned windows, prayer rugs, and soft morning sunlight.

As the teacher gently turns the mirror toward the child, something unexpected appears only inside the reflection.

Instead of reflecting the young boy exactly as he is now, the mirror reveals the same child many years in the future — now a confident, compassionate Muslim scholar and teacher standing before a beautiful library, surrounded by students learning Qur'an and Islamic knowledge.

The boy looks into the mirror with quiet wonder.

The teacher looks toward him and gives a subtle, warm smile.

The camera never enters the mirror and never cuts.

The future exists only within its reflection.

The teacher and child continue walking toward a large sunlit Islamic archway as the mirror slowly moves out of frame.

Final visual feeling:

**Today, a student. Tomorrow, a source of light for others.**
