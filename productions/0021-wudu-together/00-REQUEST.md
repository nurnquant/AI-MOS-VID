TITLE:
Wudu Together

WHO:
A calm Muslim father and his 4-year-old son.

The father wears a simple light-colored thobe.
The little boy wears comfortable modest home clothes.
Their expressions are natural, peaceful, and affectionate.

WHERE:
A clean, elegant family bathroom or wudu area just before prayer.
Soft morning or late-afternoon light enters through a nearby window.
The environment is warm, uncluttered, realistic, and family-oriented.

STORY — EXACT 10-SECOND SEQUENCE:

0–2 seconds:
Close-up of the father gently rolling up his sleeves beside the sink.

His young son watches carefully and immediately copies him, awkwardly rolling up his own tiny sleeves.

On-screen text:

“Children learn before they understand.”

2–5 seconds:
The father slowly washes his hands for wudu.

The boy watches, then carefully copies the same movement.

The father does not rush him.

He gives a small encouraging smile.

5–8 seconds:
The father gently demonstrates rinsing and washing his face.

The little boy follows with complete concentration, splashing just a little water and smiling.

Keep the moment natural, not comedic.

8–10 seconds:
Father and son stand side by side, drying their hands.

The father gently places his hand on the boy’s shoulder.

Final on-screen text:

“Teach the order. Let them copy the calm.”

Then a subtle final line:

“Riwaq Al Ilm”

VISUAL DIRECTION:

- ultra-realistic skin and fabric
- authentic water movement
- realistic hands and fingers
- gentle parent-child interaction
- warm cream, soft green, and natural wood tones
- shallow depth of field
- subtle cinematic camera movement
- no exaggerated expressions
- no fantasy effects
- no rushed editing

CAMERA:
Begin with a close-up of the sleeves.
Move into a medium side shot showing father and son mirroring each other.
Use one soft close-up during the face-washing moment.
End with a gentle pull-back showing them together.

AUDIO:

- natural running-water sounds
- light fabric movement
- subtle room ambience
- soft child breathing and small happy sounds
- no distracting music
- if background audio is used, keep it extremely gentle and secondary

EMOTIONAL INTENT:
The scene should communicate that the child is learning worship through observation before understanding every rule.

The father teaches through calm repetition, patience, and example.

The atmosphere should make wudu feel gentle, intentional, and peaceful — never rushed.

IMPORTANT:
Do not attempt to explain all fiqh rules in the video.
Do not overcrowd the scene with instructional text.
Focus on imitation, calmness, order, and the father-child bond.

ENDING:
Smooth warm fade.

Riwaq Al Ilm
Nurturing faith, knowledge & character.
