## 1/7 — سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

### Arabic

**سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ**

### Transliteration

**Subḥānallāhi wa biḥamdih.**

### English Meaning

**Glory be to Allah and praise be to Him.**

### When / Why

Recite **100 times in the morning and 100 times in the evening**. The Prophet ﷺ taught that the one who does so will not be surpassed on the Day of Resurrection except by someone who said the same or more.

### Authentic Source

Sahih Muslim; Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 3469; Riyāḍ as-Ṣāliḥīn 1451.

### Reflection

**Begin and end the day by declaring Allah’s perfection and filling your tongue with His praise.** 🌅🌙

## 2/7 — رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا

### Arabic

**رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا، وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيًّا**

### Transliteration

**Raḍītu billāhi Rabban, wa bil-Islāmi dīnan, wa bi-Muḥammadin nabiyyā.**

### English Meaning

**I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Islam as my religion, and with Muhammad ﷺ as my Prophet.**

### When / Why

A morning-and-evening remembrance renewing contentment with Allah, Islam, and the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

### Authentic Source

Sunan Ibn Mājah 3870 — graded Hasan in the cited edition.

### Reflection

**Start the day by reminding your heart who your Lord is, what your way of life is, and whose Prophetic example you follow.** ❤️

## 3/7 — بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ

### Arabic

**بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ**

### Transliteration

**Bismillāhilladhī lā yaḍurru maʿa ismihi shay’un fil-arḍi wa lā fis-samā’i, wa Huwas-Samīʿul-ʿAlīm.**

### English Meaning

**In the name of Allah, with Whose Name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.**

### When / Why

Recite **3 times in the morning and 3 times in the evening** as taught by the Prophet ﷺ, with a reported promise of protection from harm.

### Authentic Source

Sunan Ibn Mājah 3869 — Hasan; Riyāḍ as-Ṣāliḥīn 1457, citing Abū Dāwūd and at-Tirmidhī.

### Reflection

**We take sensible precautions, while our hearts recognize that true protection is ultimately from Allah.** 🤲

## 4/7 — أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ

### Arabic

**أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ**

### Transliteration

**Aʿūdhu bi kalimātillāhit-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.**

### English Meaning

**I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.**

### When / Why

Recite **3 times in the evening** as an established evening remembrance seeking Allah’s protection.

### Authentic Source

Reported in Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī and other collections; Hisn al-Muslim 97 provides supporting references and grading notes.

### Reflection

**As daylight fades, place your fears and vulnerabilities beneath the protection of Allah.** 🌙🤲

## 5/7 — اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا

### Arabic — Morning

**اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ**

### Arabic — Evening

**اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ**

### Transliteration

**Morning:** Allāhumma bika aṣbaḥnā, wa bika amsaynā, wa bika naḥyā, wa bika namūtu, wa ilaykan-nushūr.

**Evening:** Allāhumma bika amsaynā, wa bika aṣbaḥnā, wa bika naḥyā, wa bika namūtu, wa ilaykal-maṣīr.

### English Meaning

**Morning:** O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the resurrection.

**Evening:** O Allah, by You we enter the evening, by You we enter the morning, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the final return.

### When / Why

A Prophetic remembrance for entering morning and evening, acknowledging that our life, death, and return are under Allah’s authority.

### Authentic Source

At-Tirmidhī and Abū Dāwūd; Riyāḍ as-Ṣāliḥīn 1453; Hisn al-Muslim 78.

### Reflection

**Every new morning and approaching night reminds us that our entire journey belongs to Allah.** 🌅🌙

## 6/7 — اللَّهُمَّ مَا أَصْبَحَ بِي مِنْ نِعْمَةٍ

### Arabic

**اللَّهُمَّ مَا أَصْبَحَ بِي مِنْ نِعْمَةٍ أَوْ بِأَحَدٍ مِنْ خَلْقِكَ، فَمِنْكَ وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ، فَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ وَلَكَ الشُّكْرُ**

For evening, use **أَمْسَى** in place of **أَصْبَحَ**.

### Transliteration

**Allāhumma mā aṣbaḥa bī min niʿmatin aw bi-aḥadin min khalqika, fa-minka waḥdaka lā sharīka lak, fa-lakal-ḥamdu wa lakash-shukr.**

### English Meaning

**O Allah, whatever blessing I or any of Your creation have received this morning is from You alone, without partner. To You belongs all praise and all thanks.**

### When / Why

A morning remembrance acknowledging that every blessing comes from Allah; an evening form is also reported.

### Authentic Source

Abū Dāwūd, an-Nasāʾī and others; Hisn al-Muslim 81 notes a Hasan chain in its cited verification.

### Reflection

**Gratitude changes an ordinary morning: what looked routine becomes a collection of blessings from Allah.** 🌿✨

## 7/7 — Sayyid al-Istighfār

### Arabic

**اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ**

### Transliteration

**Allāhumma anta Rabbī lā ilāha illā Anta, khalaqtanī wa anā ʿabduka, wa anā ʿalā ʿahdika wa waʿdika mastataʿtu. Aʿūdhu bika min sharri mā ṣanaʿtu. Abū’u laka biniʿmatika ʿalayya, wa abū’u laka bidhanbī, faghfir lī, fa-innahu lā yaghfirudh-dhunūba illā Anta.**

### English Meaning

**O Allah, You are my Lord. None has the right to be worshipped except You. You created me and I am Your servant. I remain faithful to Your covenant and promise as much as I am able. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your blessings upon me, and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.**

### When / Why

The Prophet ﷺ described this as the foremost form of seeking forgiveness and taught a special virtue for the one who says it with certainty during the day or night.

### Authentic Source

**Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 6306** (also 6323).

### Reflection

**True istighfār joins gratitude, humility, accountability, hope, and dependence upon Allah’s forgiveness.** 🤲❤️

The Prophet ﷺ described this as the foremost supplication for seeking forgiveness.

Teach your family not only to pronounce these words, but to understand the humility, gratitude, and hope within them. 🌿❤️
