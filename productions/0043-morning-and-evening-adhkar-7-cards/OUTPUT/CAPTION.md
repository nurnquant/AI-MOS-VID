# Day 1 of 7

## Facebook

**Title**

The Easiest Hundred You Will Ever Say ✨🌙🌿

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 1 of 7

A hundred sounds like a lot until you time it.

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

Subḥānallāhi wa biḥamdih.

“Glory be to Allah and praise be to Him.”

📖 Recite 100 times in the morning and 100 times in the evening. The Prophet ﷺ taught that the one who does so will not be surpassed on the Day of Resurrection except by someone who said the same or more.

Four words, a hundred times, takes about three minutes. Most of us lose more than that deciding what to watch. And the Prophet ﷺ said the one who says it will not be surpassed on the Day of Resurrection except by someone who said the same or more.

🌿 Source: Sahih Muslim; Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 3469; Riyāḍ as-Ṣāliḥīn 1451.

# Day 2 of 7

## Facebook

**Title**

The Sentence That Answers Who You Are ❤️🌙✨

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 2 of 7

Somebody will ask your child what they are one day. This is the answer.

رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا، وَبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيًّا

Raḍītu billāhi Rabban, wa bil-Islāmi dīnan, wa bi-Muḥammadin nabiyyā.

“I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Islam as my religion, and with Muhammad ﷺ as my Prophet.”

📖 A morning-and-evening remembrance renewing contentment with Allah, Islam, and the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

It is not a slogan. It is three settled statements — my Lord, my way of life, the Prophet whose example I follow — said out loud before the day starts pulling at you.

🌿 Source: Sunan Ibn Mājah 3870 — graded Hasan in the cited edition.

# Day 3 of 7

## Facebook

**Title**

What To Say Before You Leave The House 🤲🌙✨

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 3 of 7

The school run, the commute, the door closing behind them.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Bismillāhilladhī lā yaḍurru maʿa ismihi shay’un fil-arḍi wa lā fis-samā’i, wa Huwas-Samīʿul-ʿAlīm.

“In the name of Allah, with Whose Name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.”

📖 Recite 3 times in the morning and 3 times in the evening as taught by the Prophet ﷺ, with a reported promise of protection from harm.

Three times in the morning, three in the evening. We still lock the door and check the road — and we say this, because taking precautions and trusting Allah were never two different things.

🌿 Source: Sunan Ibn Mājah 3869 — Hasan; Riyāḍ as-Ṣāliḥīn 1457, citing Abū Dāwūd and at-Tirmidhī.

## Facebook

**Title**

For The End Of A Heavy Day 🌙✨❤️

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 4 of 7

Some evenings you do not want to talk to anyone.

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

Aʿūdhu bi kalimātillāhit-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.

“I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.”

📖 Recite 3 times in the evening as an established evening remembrance seeking Allah’s protection.

Three times, as the light goes. Not a spell and not a formula — a sentence that puts what you are afraid of somewhere safer than your own hands.

🌿 Source: Reported in Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī and other collections; Hisn al-Muslim 97 provides supporting references and grading notes.

# Day 5 of 7

## Facebook

**Title**

The Whole Day, Handed Over In One Line 🌅🌙✨

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 5 of 7

By You we wake. By You we sleep. By You we live, by You we die.

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ

Allāhumma bika aṣbaḥnā, wa bika amsaynā, wa bika naḥyā, wa bika namūtu, wa ilaykan-nushūr.

“O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the resurrection.”

📖 A Prophetic remembrance for entering morning and evening, acknowledging that our life, death, and return are under Allah’s authority.

Morning and evening, with one word changed between them. It does not ask for anything. It just says out loud who the day belongs to — which is why it settles something before the day has started.

🌿 Source: At-Tirmidhī and Abū Dāwūd; Riyāḍ as-Ṣāliḥīn 1453; Hisn al-Muslim 78.

👇 Teach the morning one first. The evening one is the same sentence turned around.

**Title**

Name Three Before You Check Your Phone 🌿❤️✨

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 6 of 7

Health. Family. Faith. Food. Safety. Another morning.

اللَّهُمَّ مَا أَصْبَحَ بِي مِنْ نِعْمَةٍ أَوْ بِأَحَدٍ مِنْ خَلْقِكَ، فَمِنْكَ وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ، فَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ وَلَكَ الشُّكْرُ

Allāhumma mā aṣbaḥa bī min niʿmatin aw bi-aḥadin min khalqika, fa-minka waḥdaka lā sharīka lak, fa-lakal-ḥamdu wa lakash-shukr.

“O Allah, whatever blessing I or any of Your creation have received this morning is from You alone, without partner. To You belongs all praise and all thanks.”

📖 A morning remembrance acknowledging that every blessing comes from Allah; an evening form is also reported.

This dhikr says every blessing you woke up with came from Allah alone. The practice underneath it is simpler: before the phone, name three. Children copy that faster than they copy anything you tell them.

🌿 Source: Abū Dāwūd, an-Nasāʾī and others; Hisn al-Muslim 81 notes a Hasan chain in its cited verification.

**Title**

If You Only Memorise One, Make It This 🤲❤️🌙

**Body**

Morning & Evening Adhkār — Day 7 of 7

The Prophet ﷺ called this the foremost way of seeking forgiveness.

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

Allāhumma anta Rabbī lā ilāha illā Anta, khalaqtanī wa anā ʿabduka, wa anā ʿalā ʿahdika wa waʿdika mastataʿtu. Aʿūdhu bika min sharri mā ṣanaʿtu. Abū’u laka biniʿmatika ʿalayya, wa abū’u laka bidhanbī, faghfir lī, fa-innahu lā yaghfirudh-dhunūba illā Anta.

“O Allah, You are my Lord. None has the right to be worshipped except You. You created me and I am Your servant. I remain faithful to Your covenant and promise as much as I am able. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your blessings upon me, and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.”

📖 The Prophet ﷺ described this as the foremost form of seeking forgiveness and taught a special virtue for the one who says it with certainty during the day or night.

It is longer than the others and worth every line. Gratitude, honesty about your own sin, and hope, in one breath — you acknowledge the blessing and the failure in the same sentence, and then ask.

🌿 Source: Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 6306 (also 6323).

## Posting notes

For us, not for posting.

- **One a day, in order.** Every caption carries "Day N of 7", which is the whole
  follow mechanism — the reason to come back tomorrow. Posting them out of order
  or in a batch throws that away.
- **The framing is what makes each one different.** The Arabic is fixed, so the
  variation has to come from the opening line: the easiest hundred, what to say
  before you leave the house, for the end of a heavy day. Without that they are
  seven identical cards.
- **Day 6 has the best ask** — "what are your three this morning?" — because it
  can be answered in three words by someone who is not confident writing about
  religion.
- **Day 7 is the strongest post and the longest.** If only one is boosted, that
  one.
- **Saves matter more than likes here.** Adhkar are reference material; a parent
  saves them to use tomorrow. Consider a pinned comment saying "save this for
  your morning routine".
- **Sources stay in the caption.** They are the reason this is trustworthy rather
  than another quote graphic, and they cost one line.
