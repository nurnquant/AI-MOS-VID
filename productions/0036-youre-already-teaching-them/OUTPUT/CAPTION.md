## Facebook

You think you are not teaching your child deen.

You are. You just do not count it.

Bismillah before you left the house. They heard it.
Alhamdulillah when you were exhausted. They heard that too.
You apologised when you were wrong. No book teaches that.
Dua out loud when the news was bad. They were listening.

Here is the thing nobody says to tired parents: your child is not waiting for
the lesson to begin. They are already in it.

They did not learn to speak because you sat them down and taught them grammar.
They learned because they heard you talk, all day, every day, for years. Deen
transfers the same way. Not in the scheduled hour. In the hundred unscheduled
seconds when you were not performing anything.

Which means the days you feel you achieved nothing were not empty. You said
Bismillah. You said Alhamdulillah when you had nothing left. You said sorry to a
four-year-old, which is harder than it sounds. That was the lesson. It landed.

You do not need to be a scholar to raise one. You need to be heard.

Say the words out loud today, where they can hear you. That is the whole
instruction.

Which one do you catch yourself saying most in front of them? 👇
