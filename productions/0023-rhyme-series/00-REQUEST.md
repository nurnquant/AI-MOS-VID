# Riwaq Al Ilm --- Children's Rhyme Series

## Series Vision

A recurring children's Islamic rhyme series for Riwaq Al Ilm, designed
for short-form social video and longer compilation videos.

**Primary audience:** Muslim children and families\
**Primary format:** 10-second video clips\
**Full rhyme format:** 40--60 seconds\
**Video aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical\
**Platforms:** Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Stories\
**Style:** Warm, joyful, educational, family-friendly, and rooted in
Islamic character

## Production System

Each rhyme can follow this structure:

1.  4--6 short verses
2.  One 10-second Google Flow video per verse
3.  Consistent recurring child characters
4.  Simple, memorable melody and rhythm
5.  Clear Islamic or educational lesson
6.  Full 40--60 second compilation
7.  Smooth, warm **Riwaq Al Ilm** ending

# Content Scale

The series provides:

- **30 complete children's rhymes**
- **5 thematic seasons**
- **4--6 verses per rhyme**
- Approximately **120--180 individual 10-second videos**
- 30 potential 40--60 second compilation Reels/Shorts
- Reusable clips for Stories, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube

## Recommended Naming Convention

```text
CR-S01-E01 — Bismillah, Let's Learn Today
CR-S01-E02 — Thank You, Allah
CR-S02-E01 — Alif, Baa, Taa Adventure
```

For individual verse assets:

```text
CR-S01-E01-V01
CR-S01-E01-V02
CR-S01-E01-V03
```

This structure keeps Google Flow prompts, generated videos, captions,
thumbnails, and final compilation assets easy to organize.

# Riwaq Al Ilm Production Principles

- Keep language simple enough for young children.
- Make every verse easy to sing and remember.
- Maintain careful Arabic pronunciation.
- Verify Quranic, dua, and religious wording before publication.
- Keep visuals modest, warm, joyful, and age-appropriate.
- Prefer recurring characters to build familiarity.
- Avoid overwhelming scenes and excessive on-screen text.
- Preserve consistent visual identity across episodes.
- End each completed video smoothly and warmly with **Riwaq Al Ilm**.
