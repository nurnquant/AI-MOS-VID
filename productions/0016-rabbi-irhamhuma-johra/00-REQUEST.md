WHO

Johra, a 5-year-old Muslim girl with a beige khimar and emerald-green dress, and her loving mother wearing a cream-colored hijab.

WHERE

A warm, softly lit bedroom at night. Moonlight enters through the window, illuminating Islamic decorations and a small Quran on the bedside table.

WHAT MOVES

Johra finishes reciting:

"رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا"

She walks to her mother, hugs her tightly, and rests her head on her mother's shoulder. The mother smiles, gently kisses Johra's forehead, and tucks her into bed.

HOW CAMERA MOVES

Start with a medium shot of Johra sitting on the bed. Slowly dolly in as she recites the dua, then transition to a close-up of the hug. End with a soft overhead shot of mother and daughter.

HOW IT ENDS

The screen gently fades to dark green as golden Arabic calligraphy appears:

"رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا"

Below it, the English translation appears:

"My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small."
