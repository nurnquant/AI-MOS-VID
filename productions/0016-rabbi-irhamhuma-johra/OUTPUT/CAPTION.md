رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

We spend years raising them, holding them, praying over them... and then one night, they raise their little hands and pray for us.

Teach your child this dua, and you have given yourself a gift that will outlive you.
