🎬 Title: "Alif, Baa, Taa Adventure"

WHO

Johra, a joyful 5-year-old Muslim girl wearing a beige khimar and an emerald-green dress, sits with three friends in their Islamic classroom. The children are excited, focused, and eager to learn Arabic together.

WHERE

A warm and colorful classroom filled with Arabic alphabet posters, bookshelves, stars, and crescent moon decorations. Morning sunlight shines through the windows, creating a magical learning atmosphere.

SONG LYRICS (Verse 2)

🎵

Tiny fingers point and trace,

Big smiles on every face.

Word by word and line by line,

🎵

WHAT HAPPENS

Johra uses her tiny finger to trace the Arabic letters ا, ب, and ت in her colorful workbook. Her eyes light up with excitement.

2–4 seconds

The camera moves across the classroom, showing all the children smiling as they point to different Arabic words in their books.

4–7 seconds

Johra's teacher gently helps the children pronounce the letters. The children repeat together:

"Alif, Baa, Taa!"

As they speak, the letters softly glow above the pages.

7–10 seconds

Johra proudly reads a simple word from the book while her friends clap and cheer. The camera slowly pulls back, revealing the joyful classroom.
