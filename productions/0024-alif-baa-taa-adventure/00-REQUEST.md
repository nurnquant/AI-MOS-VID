# 🌟 Alif, Baa, Taa — Come Learn With Me!

**Style:** Cheerful children's sing-along
**Age:** 3–8
**Length:** ~50–60 seconds
**Theme:** Learning the first Arabic letters

### 🎵 Intro

Come along, come sing with me,
Arabic is fun, you'll see!
Clap your hands — one, two, three,
Learn the letters happily! 🎶

### 🎵 Verse 1 — Alif

**Alif, Alif — ا!**
Alif stands up tall!

Straight and strong, look and see,
**Alif — ا**, say it with me!

**Alif! Alif! ا!** 🌟

### 🎵 Verse 2 — Baa

**Baa, Baa — ب!**
One little dot below!

One dot down, now you know,
**Baa — ب**, let's say it slow!

**Baa! Baa! ب!** 🔵

### 🎵 Verse 3 — Taa

**Taa, Taa — ت!**
Two little dots on top!

One, two — there they are!
**Taa — ت**, you're a little star!

**Taa! Taa! ت!** ⭐

### 🎵 Verse 4 — Saa

**Saa, Saa — ث!**
Three little dots on top!

Count them with me:

**One! Two! Three!**

**Saa — ث**, as easy as can be!

**Saa! Saa! ث!** ✨

### 🎵 Sing-Along Chorus

**Alif — ا!**
**Baa — ب!**
**Taa — ت!**
**Saa — ث!**

Alif, Baa, Taa, Saa,
Arabic letters — **Mā shā’ Allāh!**

Clap your hands and sing with me,
Learning Arabic happily! ❤️

### 🎵 Ending

Alif, Baa, Taa, Saa,
Look how much we learned today!

Little steps along the way,
We'll learn some more another day! 🌙

**Riwaq Al Ilm**
_Learn • Grow • Love the Qur'an_
