# Allah Loves When I Do Good

_Image post · style 5 · concept given at high level, detail drafted here_

## Concept

Good character, at a child's scale. Not "be a good Muslim" as an abstraction —
one small kind act a five-year-old could do this afternoon, and the knowledge
that Allah notices it.

## Scene

A sunny garden courtyard outside a family home, late morning. Warm light, green
grass, flowers along a low wall.

**The main act, centred:** a young Muslim boy about five, in a light blue kurta,
crouched down and holding out half an orange to a smaller child who has none.
Both are smiling — the giving is easy and happy, not solemn.

**A second act, softer, to one side:** a girl in a mustard dress setting down a
small bowl of water for a thirsty kitten, which is drinking.

Generous open sky across the top. Lower-left corner deliberately clear.

The feeling: _"doing good is small, and Allah sees it."_

## Type

Title: **🌟 ALLAH LOVES WHEN I DO GOOD 💛**

Couplet:

> Share a snack, help someone stand —
> Allah loves a helping hand!

## Constraints

- No depiction of Allah in any form. Allah is present only through the children's
  actions.
- No text generated in the image; all type composited locally.
- Keep the lower-left corner uncluttered for the logo.
- Nothing that reads as commerce — no coins, no money boxes.
