## Verse 2 — Park (`Child_gratitude_park_RiwaqAlIlm.mp4`)

Grandfather's hand. A teacher's patience. Friends who share their apples. 🌳

🎵 For my parents, kind and true,
For my teachers helping too.
For my friends who laugh and share,
Thank You, Allah, everywhere. 🎵

We teach children to thank Allah for food and sunshine — but the biggest
blessings in a child's life are usually people.

The grandparent who walks slowly so they can keep up. The teacher who explains
it a fourth time. The friend who hands over half.

Gratitude fills every heart. ❤️
