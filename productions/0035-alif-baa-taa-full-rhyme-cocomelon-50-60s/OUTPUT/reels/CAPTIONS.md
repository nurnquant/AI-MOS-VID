## Reel 3 — Baa · 0035-reel-3-baa-9x16.mp4

ب Baa has ONE dot below 👇

One finger, pointing down. That is the whole trick — a two-year-old cannot read a
dot yet, but they can copy a hand.

Do it with them once and watch them do it back.

Letter 2 of 4 👇

## Reel 4 — Taa · 0035-reel-4-taa-9x16.mp4

ت Taa has TWO dots on top ✌️

Two fingers up. That is it. This is the one most children copy first, because two
fingers is the easiest shape a small hand can make.

Try it — we are betting yours gets this one before the others.

## Reel 5 — Saa · 0035-reel-5-saa-9x16.mp4

## Posting notes

These notes are for us, not for posting.

- One a day, in order. Each ends with "letter N of 4", which gives a reason to come
  back tomorrow — that is what turns a viewer into a follower.
- Reels 2 to 5 open with the letter card already popping, which is a strong first
  frame. Reel 1 opens on a wider shot and is the weakest hook of the five; if only
  one gets posted, do not make it that one.
- Post the full 52 s version last, as "the whole song", to catch anyone who liked a
  single letter.
- Cross-post every one to Instagram Reels. Same file, same caption.
- Sound on matters. Consider a "sound on" sticker on the first frame.
- Reply to comments in the first hour. Early engagement decides how far a Reel goes.
