## Facebook

Three words. Three situations. That is the whole of it.

When life is easy — Alhamdulillah.
When life is difficult — Sabr.
And in every moment in between — Tawakkul.

Most of us have one setting. Things go well and we barely notice; things go
badly and we come apart. What this gives you is a different response for each,
and none of them is pretending.

Alhamdulillah is not "everything is fine". It is noticing, out loud, that
something good came from somewhere.

Sabr is not silence. It is holding steady while it hurts, without deciding that
Allah has abandoned you.

Tawakkul is the one people find hardest, because it is not sitting still. It is
doing everything you can and then genuinely letting go of the part that was never
in your hands.

Here is the line the video runs out of time to say:

Because the believer knows that whatever Allah chooses is always best for him.

That is the reason all three work. Not optimism. Not pretending. A settled
conviction that the One choosing knows more than you do.

Three Arabic words your child can learn this week, and use for the rest of their
life.

Spoken by Ustadh Abdul Baset Nadim, one of our teachers. The same voice your
child would learn from.

## Posting notes

For us, not for posting.

- **The credit is worded as the VOICE, deliberately: "spoken by", not "featuring".**
  The footage looks generated, so the face on screen is very likely not his. A
  bare name under a video of a man implies that man is him. "Spoken by" is
  accurate either way and protects him from being visually misidentified.
  **If the face IS him, say so and it can be reworded** — that would be a
  stronger post, not a weaker one.
- **Consistent with 0039 and 0040**, which credit the same teacher for the
  recitations. The full name is used every time: "Abdul Baset" alone collides
  with Abdul Basit Abdus Samad, one of the most famous reciters in the world.
- **The video stops before the last line, and the caption finishes it.** He says
  three principles and never reaches the reason. Rather than hide that, the
  caption carries "because the believer knows that whatever Allah chooses is
  always best for him" as its turn. **If a second clip is ever made with that
  line, rewrite the caption** — the structure only works while the line is
  missing from the picture.
- **The definitions are the substance.** Saying what each word is NOT — not
  "everything is fine", not silence, not sitting still — is what stops this being
  another three-Arabic-words post. Keep them if the copy is cut.
- **The ask is real and answerable.** "Which of the three is hardest for you?"
  costs nothing to answer, and most people will say Tawakkul, which makes the
  replies agree with each other and keeps the thread warm.
- Do not post adjacent to 0039 or 0040. Same audience slot, and those two are
  recitation.
