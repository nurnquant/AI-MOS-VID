## Facebook

**Title**

The Two Minutes Most of Us Skip 🤲🌙✨❤️

**Body**

The prayer ends and the phone comes out.

Almost all of us do it. The salām is barely finished and we are already reaching
for something. But the Prophet ﷺ did not stand up straight after the prayer — he
stayed, and he said these.

🤲 Astaghfirullāh, Astaghfirullāh, Astaghfirullāh.

Three times. Asking forgiveness for a prayer you just offered — because you know
how much of it your mind was somewhere else.

🌙 Allāhumma Antas-Salāmu wa minkas-salām, tabārakta yā Dhal-Jalāli wal-Ikrām.

O Allah, You are Peace, and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, Owner of
Majesty and Honour.

✨ Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamd, wa Huwa
ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr.

There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion, His is all
praise, and He is able to do all things.

📖 That is the whole of it. Under a minute, five times a day.

It is also the easiest thing to teach a child, because they are already sitting
there. They have just prayed with you, they have nowhere to be, and the words
repeat. 🧒 A six-year-old who hears you say these every day will have them without
ever being made to memorise anything.

Do not stand up straight away tomorrow. Stay for one more minute.

👇 Be honest — do you say these after every salah, or do you mean to?

## Posting notes

For us, not for posting.

- **GATE: the imagery needs a qualified look first.** A robed bearded man on a
  white horse moving toward a luminous tree reads, to a meaningful part of this
  audience, as an attempt at the Prophet ﷺ or at the Isrāʾ and Miʿrāj. The
  caption cannot fix that; only a decision about the video can. See 01-NOTES.md.
- **The hook is a confession, not an instruction.** "The prayer ends and the
  phone comes out" is true of nearly everyone reading it, which is why it earns
  the next line. Starting with "the sunnah is to say" would lose them.
- **The transliteration is verbatim from the video's subtitles**, so what is read
  in the caption matches what is heard and shown. Do not retype it from memory.
- **ﷺ is used after the Prophet's name** and should stay in every version.
- **The ask is deliberately uncomfortable and easy to answer**: "do you say
  these, or do you mean to?" Most people mean to. That is a reply people are
  willing to make because it is not a claim of piety.
- **It is 1920x1080 landscape.** Feed wants 4:5, Reels wants 9:16. Whatever is
  decided about the imagery, the shape still has to be solved before posting.
