## Facebook

Count them.

Muslim men and Muslim women.
Believing men and believing women.
Devoutly obedient men and devoutly obedient women.
Truthful men and truthful women.
Patient men and patient women.
Humble men and humble women.
Charitable men and charitable women.
Fasting men and fasting women.
The men who guard their chastity, and the women who do so.
The men who remember Allah often, and the women who do so.

Ten qualities. Each one named twice — once for the men, once for the women. Not
summarised. Not implied. Said out loud, twenty times, in a single verse.

And then one promise, for all of them together: forgiveness, and a great reward.

There is no version of this verse where one half of the room is being spoken to
and the other half is being spoken about.

That is worth sitting with, and it is worth teaching. When your daughter asks
whether this is for her too, you do not need to explain or soften anything. You
can simply read her the verse and let her count.

— Qur'an, Surah Al-Ahzab 33:35

## Posting notes

For us, not for posting.

- **HARD GATE: the reciter of this recording is not named.** Unlike 0039, which
  is Abdul Baset, this file arrived with no attribution. Name him before posting,
  and credit him in the caption the way 0039 does — it is the strongest line
  available, because the voice belongs to a teacher a parent could book.
- **Post the right cut to the right place.** 4:5 to feed, 9:16 to Reels. They are
  laid out differently on purpose: the feed cut shows the Arabic and the
  translation together, the Reel alternates them because twelve lines will not fit
  above the platform UI.
- **The caption stays with what the verse plainly says.** The well-known report
  that this verse was revealed after Umm Salamah asked why the Qur'an addresses
  men would make the post considerably stronger — but a narration in a caption
  needs a verified source and a sign-off, and neither is on file. Add it only if
  someone qualified confirms the wording and the reference.
- **The listing IS the hook.** Ten pairs read as a list is the most striking thing
  about this verse, so the caption prints them rather than describing them. Long
  for social, and worth it — the repetition is the argument.
- **The engagement ask is deliberately absent.** No "comment ameen", no question.
  The closing line about a daughter counting for herself is the whole ask.
- Do not post adjacent to 0039, the other recitation piece. Same format, same
  audience slot.
