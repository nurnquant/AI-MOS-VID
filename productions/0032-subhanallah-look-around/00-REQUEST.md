# SubhanAllah, Look Around

_Image post · style 5 · concept given at high level, detail drafted here_

## Concept

**Look CLOSER, not wider.** This is deliberately the opposite of 0027 "Allah Made
Everything", which is a wide landscape — sky, mountains, river, a child taking in
all of creation at once.

SubhanAllah is the word of astonishment at how one thing is _made_. So this post
goes down to the ground and looks at a single small creature. The argument for the
caption: subhanallah is not reserved for sunsets and mountains. It belongs to the
ladybird's wing, the ant carrying a crumb four times its size, the dew on a web.

Awe does not need scale.

## Scene

Low to the ground in a sunlit garden, camera down at grass level.

**The main beat, centred:** a young Muslim girl about five lies propped on her
elbows on the grass, chin near the ground, holding a magnifying glass over a broad
green leaf. A bright red ladybird with black spots sits on the leaf, hugely
detailed and clear. Her eye is wide with delight through the glass — the
expression is _"look at THAT"_.

**Small wonders around her, all close and detailed:** a snail with a spiral shell,
a spider web strung between two stems beaded with dew catching the light, an ant
carrying a crumb, a few tiny wildflowers, a beetle with a shiny back.

Background: the top third soft, **bright and out of focus** — sunlit greenery and
warm light bokeh, pale enough for a title to sit on.

Lower-left corner deliberately clear.

The feeling: _"the small things are the astonishing ones."_

## Type

Title: **🔍 SUBHANALLAH, LOOK AROUND! 🐞**

Couplet:

> A tiny wing, a drop of dew —
> SubhanAllah, what a view!

## Constraints

- No depiction of Allah in any form.
- No text generated in the image; all type composited locally.
- Keep the lower-left corner uncluttered for the logo.
- **Close and macro, not a landscape.** No mountains, no wide vista, no big sky —
  that is 0027's picture, not this one.
- Insects must be friendly and appealing, never creepy — this is for ages 3–8.
