# Alhamdulillah Every Day

_Image post · style 5 · concept given at high level, detail drafted here_

## Concept

The **daily repeat**. Deliberately distinct from 0028 "Thank You, Allah", which is
an evening, indoor, still moment on a prayer mat. This one is **morning**, active,
and about starting the same way again — today, tomorrow, and on the days that are
not good ones.

The theology worth carrying into the caption: alhamdulillah is not only for good
days. It is the same word on hard days. That is what makes it _every_ day.

## Scene

A child's bedroom just after waking, early sunrise light.

**The main beat, centred:** a young Muslim boy about five stands at an open window
with both arms stretched wide in a big happy morning stretch, chin lifted, eyes
half-closed in a contented smile — the first breath of a new day.

**Signs the day is starting again:** his bed just behind him with the covers
thrown back, two small birds perched on the windowsill, a potted plant on the sill
with one new bud opening, slippers waiting on the floor, warm sunrise sky outside
with soft pink and gold clouds.

Generous clear wall across the top. Lower-left corner deliberately clear.

The feeling: _"same words, new day."_

## Type

Title: **☀️ ALHAMDULILLAH EVERY DAY 🐦**

Couplet:

> Good days, hard days, all the way —
> Alhamdulillah every day!

## Constraints

- No depiction of Allah in any form.
- No text generated in the image, and **no numbers** — no calendars, no clocks
  with figures. All type composited locally.
- Keep the lower-left corner uncluttered for the logo.
- Morning, not evening. Must not read as a bedtime scene.
