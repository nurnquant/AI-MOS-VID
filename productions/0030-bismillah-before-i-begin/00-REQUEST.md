# Bismillah Before I Begin

_Image post · style 5 · concept given at high level, detail drafted here_

## Concept

The **pause** before starting. Not bismillah before eating — 0019 already covered
food. This is bismillah before _anything_: opening a book, going out of the door,
starting a task. The teachable moment is the half-second of stopping.

## Scene

A child's bright study nook at a low wooden desk, warm morning light through the
window.

**The main beat, centred:** a young Muslim girl about five sits at the desk with a
closed book in front of her, both hands resting flat on the cover, eyes gently
closed, lips just parted — saying bismillah before she opens it. Calm, unhurried,
a small private moment.

**Signs of "about to begin" around her, none of them started yet:** sharpened
pencils lined up, a closed pencil case, a satchel packed and standing ready, a
glass of milk untouched, small shoes placed neatly by the door.

Generous clear wall across the top. Lower-left corner deliberately clear.

The feeling: _"stop for one second, then start."_

## Type

Title: **🤲 BISMILLAH BEFORE I BEGIN 🌟**

Couplet:

> Big or small, before I start —
> Bismillah, with all my heart!

## Constraints

- No depiction of Allah in any form.
- No text generated in the image — no lettering on the book, the wall, the pencil
  case or anywhere else. All type composited locally.
- Keep the lower-left corner uncluttered for the logo.
- The book stays **closed**; the moment is before beginning, not during.
