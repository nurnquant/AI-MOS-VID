🤲 **BISMILLAH BEFORE I BEGIN** 🌟

_Big or small, before I start —_
_Bismillah, with all my heart!_

Most of us taught our children **Bismillah before eating**. Good. But that leaves
them thinking it is a food word.

It is a **starting** word.

Before homework. Before shoes go on. Before the car door shuts. Before they climb
into bed. Anything with a beginning has room for it.

And here is why it is worth the effort while they are small: a child who pauses
before starting is learning two things at once. That Allah is with them in
ordinary moments, not only in the difficult ones. And **how to stop for a second
before rushing in** — which is a gift they will need at seven, and at seventeen.

You do not need to teach it. Say it out loud yourself, every time, where they can
hear you. Before you start the car. Before you open the laptop. They will pick it
up the way they picked up everything else — by watching.

Small pause. Big habit.

## Posting notes

- **The hook is the correction**, not the scene: "your child thinks Bismillah is a
  food word." It reframes something almost every parent in the audience has
  already half-taught, which is why it earns a comment.
- **The method is modelling, not instruction** — "say it yourself where they can
  hear you." Costs a parent nothing and can start today.
- **The engagement ask is binary** ("food only, or everywhere?") — the lowest
  possible effort to answer, and the answers self-sort the audience.
- The image is deliberately pre-action: closed book, untouched milk, packed bag,
  shoes still by the door. If anyone asks why nothing is happening — that is the
  point, and it is worth saying in a reply.
- Fourth in the sequence: **0027** creation → **0028** gratitude → **0029**
  character → **0030** beginning things with Allah's name.
- Distinct from **0019** "Bismillah Before We Eat" — that one is food, this one is
  everything else. They cross-promote well.
