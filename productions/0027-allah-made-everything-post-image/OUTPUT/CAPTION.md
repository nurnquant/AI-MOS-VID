## Facebook

"Look — birds!" 🐦

That is where tawheed starts. Not with a definition. With a child noticing
something and being told who made it.

The sun that warms them. The moon that shows up in a morning sky and confuses
them. The stream. The lamb. The butterfly that will not sit still.

Allah made every single one.

🌎 ALLAH MADE EVERYTHING 🌿

The sun and moon, the birds and trees —
Allah made them all for us to see!

Here is something you can do today, and it costs nothing. On your next walk, let
your child pick one thing — a leaf, an ant, a cloud — and say together:
"Alhamdulillah, Allah made this."

Do it enough times and something settles in them. The world stops being scenery.
It becomes a signed painting, and they know the Artist's name.

That is the first aqeedah lesson, and you are the one who teaches it. Long before
any classroom.

## Posting notes

- Works muted and thumbnail-small — the title is baked in, so the post still
  says what it is at feed scale.
- The engagement ask is deliberately tiny ("the smallest thing"). Low effort
  to answer, and every answer is a proud parent moment, which is what gets replies.
- The takeaway is an action, not a sentiment. "Pick one thing on your walk and
  say Alhamdulillah" is repeatable tomorrow — that is what makes a post get saved
  and shared rather than just liked.
- Pinterest copy is written as search text, not as a caption — Pinterest is a
  search engine, so it leads with the topic and keeps the phrasing plain.
- Pair with 0026 if a two-image carousel is wanted; same topic, single child.
