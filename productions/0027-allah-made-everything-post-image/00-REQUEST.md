For an image representing “Allah Made Everything,” I would make it joyful, colorful, and immediately understandable to a young child:

Scene: A beautiful early-morning meadow viewed from a child’s perspective. Two happy Muslim children—a young boy and girl in modest, colorful clothing—stand together looking with wonder at the world around them.

The scene includes a bright blue sky, fluffy clouds, warm sunshine, green hills, colorful flowers, butterflies, birds flying overhead, a gentle stream, trees, and small friendly animals such as a rabbit and lamb. In the distance are peaceful mountains and a crescent-shaped moon faintly visible in the morning sky.

The children point excitedly toward different parts of creation—the sun, birds, flowers, trees, and animals—as though discovering them one by one.

The overall feeling should communicate:

“Look around! Allah made all these wonderful things.”

For the visual style, use a premium preschool 3D animated aesthetic: soft rounded characters, expressive faces, bright but harmonious colors, warm sunlight, playful movement, and a wholesome family-friendly atmosphere.

The main title could be:

🌎 ALLAH MADE EVERYTHING 🌿

With smaller text:

“The sun and moon, the birds and trees—
Allah made them all for us to see!”

I would not depict Allah in any form. Allah is represented only through the beauty and diversity of His creation.
