## Facebook

There are days when you cannot fix it. 🤲

Not the situation. Not the person. Not even yourself.

And on those days there is a sentence the Prophet ﷺ called a treasure:

**لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ**
_Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh_

"There is no power and no strength except through Allah."

Listen to what you are actually saying:

**Lā ḥawla** — I have no ability to turn away from hardship, from sin, from
difficulty, except with Your help.

**Wa lā quwwata** — and I have no strength to do what is good, except through
You.

**Illā billāh** — except by Allah.

It is not a sentence of defeat. It is where the weight goes when it is too heavy
for you. You are not being asked to be strong enough. You are being asked to
stop carrying it alone.

Say it when the news breaks. Say it when your patience runs out at 4pm. Say it
when you know the right thing to do and cannot make yourself do it.

O Allah — guide us to what is right, give us strength for what pleases You, and
keep our hearts firm on the straight path.

If this reached you today, say **Ameen** 🤍 and send it to someone carrying
something heavy.

## Posting notes

- **58 seconds** — fits Reels, TikTok and Shorts without a cut. Facebook feed is
  fine; for Stories it needs splitting into two.
- **Sound on matters.** The narration carries the entire piece. Worth an overlay
  or first comment saying "sound on 🔊" — this one is meaningless muted.
- **Best time to post:** the emotional register is evening/late-night, not
  morning. Same audience that saves dhikr posts at 10pm.
- **Ameen is the engagement ask** and it is already on screen at the end, so the
  caption's ask and the video's ask reinforce rather than compete.
- The dua appears on screen in Arabic at three fragment beats plus the full dua
  at the open, so the Arabic is readable even for viewers who don't know it by
  ear. Harakat are stripped (host font limitation) — the transliteration carries
  the vowelling.

## Production notes

- **All Veo clip audio was discarded.** 6 of 9 clips came back with a music bed
  (−17 to −23 dB) despite "ABSOLUTELY NO MUSIC" pinned in every prompt. The
  soundtrack is built locally: one continuous Emmett read + Apple-DLS sampled
  piano at 45% + rain ambience lifted from the one verified-clean clip.

  "quwwata" in 7 attempts ("quata", "kuwata", "kuwatha", "WALA KUATA"). Same for
