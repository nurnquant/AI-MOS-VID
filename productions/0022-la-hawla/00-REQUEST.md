VIDEO NARRATION — LA HAWLA WA LA QUWWATA ILLA BILLAH

Arabic:
لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

Transliteration:
Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.

Narration:

“Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.”

Recite these beautiful words—and reflect on their meaning.

“Lā ḥawla” —
O Allah, I have no ability to turn away from hardship, sin, or difficulty except with Your help.

“Wa lā quwwata” —
And I have no strength to accomplish what is good except through You.

“Illā billāh” —
Except by Allah.

O Allah, guide me toward what is right.
Give me strength to do what pleases You.
And keep my heart firm upon the straight path.

“Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.”

There is no power and no strength except through Allah.

If this reminder touched your heart, say “Ameen,” and share it with someone you love.
