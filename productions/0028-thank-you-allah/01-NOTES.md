## The brief

The user gave the concept only — **"Thank You, Allah"** — and said to do the rest.
So the scene, title, couplet and framing are mine, written into
`00-REQUEST.md` before generating so the request is on record rather than implied.

Angle chosen: gratitude at a child's scale. Not a grand blessing — the ordinary
ones a five-year-old can actually see and name, because that is what makes the
caption's bedtime habit ("three things, smallest first") land.

## What worked, first try

- Every briefed element is present: child on a prayer mat with cupped hands, fruit
  bowl, glass of water, sleeping cat, flowering plant, folded blanket, Qur'an
  stand, sunset window with a crescent moon.
- **The prompt asked for a deliberately uncluttered lower-left corner**, and the
  model obliged. The logo landed on clear floor with nothing to collide with —
  the first time that has not needed a fix. This instruction is now worth carrying
  in every prompt, and has been added to the skill.
- No text anywhere in the render. No divine depiction; gratitude is carried by the
  child and the objects.

## Fixed during composition

- **🤍 white heart was near-invisible** against a peach wall. Swapped to 🌸, which
  also matches the flowers and the coral dress. Emoji contrast against the actual
  background now belongs in the check list.

## Known, accepted

- **The model put a hard horizontal seam at exactly y=1024** — a flat wall band
  across the top quarter, edge-detected as a 2.0-brightness step at precisely
  ¼ of the frame, so it is a generation artefact rather than a ceiling. It is
  useful: the title sits in it and it now reads as a deliberate header band above
  the curtains. Left as is rather than spending another 4 credits.
- **9:16 shows two stacked bands** — the blurred fill band, then the image's own
  wall band — because this particular source already has a flat top quarter. Reads
  as wall above a valance at phone size. Fixing it properly would mean trimming
  the source band before padding, which the generic script does not do per-ratio.
- The Qur'an cover carries an embossed medallion. Checked at full resolution: it
  is abstract ornament, not garbled lettering.

## Verified

Every one of the five ratios opened and looked at, plus an enlarged crop of the
logo corner. Checks: all briefed elements still visible, no text clipped at an
edge, logo clear of subjects, title legible over what sits behind it. The 16:9
couplet moved to the right of frame as the skill prescribes, clear of the child.
