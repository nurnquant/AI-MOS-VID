# Thank You, Allah

_Image post · style 5 · concept given at high level, detail drafted here_

## Concept

Gratitude, at a child's scale. Not a grand blessing — the ordinary ones a small
child can actually see and name.

## Scene

A cosy family room in warm golden evening light. A young Muslim child sits back
on their heels on a small prayer mat, hands open in front of them, eyes closed,
smiling — saying thank you.

Arranged around them, the everyday blessings a child would notice: a bowl of
fruit, a glass of water, a sleeping cat, a potted plant in flower, a folded
blanket, a wooden Qur'an stand. Through the window, a sunset sky and a slim
crescent moon.

Warm, safe, unhurried. The feeling is _"look how much I already have."_

## Type

Title: **🤲 THANK YOU, ALLAH 🤍**

Couplet:

> For every gift, both big and small —
> Thank you, Allah, for it all!

## Constraints

- No depiction of Allah in any form. Allah is present only through blessings.
- No text generated in the image; all type composited locally.
- Keep the lower-left corner uncluttered for the logo.
