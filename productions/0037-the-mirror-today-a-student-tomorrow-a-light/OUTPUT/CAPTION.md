## Posting notes

For us, not for posting.

- **The whole video is one 8 s shot.** The hook is visual and needs no text: a
  mirror that refuses to reflect. It reads with sound off, which matters because
  there is no voiceover to lose.
- **The engagement ask is the strongest thing here.** "Who taught you? Most of
  them never got thanked" invites a name, costs nothing to answer, and every
  answer is a small tribute — that is the kind of comment thread that keeps
  running for days.
- The word **riwaq** is explained inside the caption. It is the company's own
  name and most of the audience does not know what it means; explaining it makes
  the brand mean something instead of just being a label.
- **No music anywhere**, by the brief's instruction. The end card is carried by
  the film's own room tone.
- Pairs with 0036: that one tells a parent they are already teaching, this one
  tells them what their child will become. **Do not post them adjacent** — a week
