# The Mirror — Today a Student, Tomorrow a Light

_Production 0037 · type: video · approved 2026-08-14_

Brief as written by the user: [00-REQUEST-original.md](00-REQUEST-original.md).
Delivered as a **single 8 s take plus a brand end card**, 10.6 s total.

## What changed from the brief, and why

The brief was reviewed and tested before anything was committed —
`work/mockups/FINDINGS.md` is the record of 64 credits spent proving the concept.
Four deliberate departures:

**1. The setting moved from a decorated hall to a riwaq colonnade.** Two tests
showed the model will not stop generating pseudo-Arabic on the walls of a
decorated Islamic interior — it is what those buildings look like in its training
data, and eight separate prohibitions did not remove it. A plain stone colonnade
has no calligraphic tradition to imitate. **The delivered frame contains no text
of any kind**, verified by enlarging the shelves, the book covers, the columns and
the open book inside the mirror, whose pages came back blank.

It also happens to be the more honest setting: _riwaq_ is the company's name.

**2. One take, not the full walk.** The brief asks for a single uninterrupted take
and then describes 30-40 s of action. Omni caps at 10 s. Rather than cut the film
into four — which the brief explicitly does not want — the action was cut to the
one beat that carries the idea: the mirror turns, the future appears, the teacher
looks at the boy and smiles.

**3. Medium-wide instead of wide.** A wide tracking two-shot fights 9:16; at that
ratio you keep the architecture or the faces, not both.

**4. "16mm film" was dropped from the prompt.** Veo rendered it literally, burning
sprocket holes and frame numbers into the picture. Asking for the qualities — soft
grain, gentle halation, restrained contrast — gives the look without the artefact.

## Sound

**No music, per the brief.** Only the take's own ambience: courtyard birds, fabric,
stone. The end card is carried by the film's own room tone, looped and faded, so
the brief's sound design survives and the film still does not end in silence.
