TITLE: The Most Beautiful Sound

DURATION: ~50 seconds

FORMAT: Vertical 9:16

STYLE: Ultra-realistic cinematic film, warm Islamic atmosphere, premium
family-commercial quality, soft golden lighting, shallow depth of field.

SOURCE: Woven from the six themes of fbPostRequest/series01.md — a
mother's dua, the first word "Iqra", true success, the most beautiful
sound, one dua, and the last thing a child hears before sleep.

WHO

- Mother: Middle-Eastern woman, early 30s, elegant cream hijab, gentle
  tired eyes full of love.
- Daughter: girl age 6, "Maryam", curly dark hair, soft lilac pajamas.
- Home: warm modern apartment, emerald-and-gold accents, lamp light,
  Quran on a wooden shelf.

STORY

Scene 1 (0–7) — A Mother's Dua
Night. The mother sits beside Maryam's bed while she sleeps, raises her
hands and whispers a dua over her. Inner-voice VO (English): "Every
night I ask Allah to guide her... but who will teach her His words?"

Scene 2 (7–14) — The First Word
Flash of warm morning. Maryam, curious, pulls the Quran from the shelf
and climbs onto the couch with it. She looks up at her mother.
Maryam (English): "Mama, what was the first word Allah sent down?"
Mother, smiling softly: "Iqra... Read."

Scene 3 (14–21) — The Question of Success
The mother watches Maryam trace the page with her small finger.
Inner-voice VO: "I dream of her success in everything... but true
success begins with a heart connected to Allah."

Scene 4 (21–29) — Learning
Warm evening. Maryam at the desk with headphones, a kind teacher on the
laptop screen; the mother watches from the doorway. Maryam repeats
letters softly, concentrating, then smiles at a small victory.
(Teacher speech soft/indistinct under music — no pinned dialogue.)

Scene 5 (29–38) — The Most Beautiful Sound
Golden hour. Maryam sits cross-legged on the couch, closes her eyes,
and recites clearly and slowly — the ONLY Arabic in the film:
"Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Raheem. Alhamdu lillahi Rabbil-'alameen."
The mother stops in the hallway, hand over her heart, eyes filling.
VO (English, whispered): "This... is the most beautiful sound a parent
can ever hear."

Scene 6 (38–46) — Before She Sleeps
Night again — mirror of Scene 1. Mother and Maryam sit together on the
bed, reciting quietly from one small Quran, the crescent night lamp
glowing. Maryam's eyes grow heavy; the mother kisses her forehead.
VO: "Stories fade. Toys break. But the words of Allah remain forever."
