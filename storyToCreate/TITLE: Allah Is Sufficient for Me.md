TITLE: Allah Is Sufficient for Me

DURATION: 10 seconds

FORMAT: Vertical (9:16)

STYLE: Ultra-realistic cinematic film, warm Islamic atmosphere, emotional and heartwarming.

WHO

A cute 6-year-old Muslim girl named Johra, wearing a beige hijab and an emerald-green dress.

WHERE

A school playground in the afternoon.

STORY

0–2 seconds

Johra stands alone in the school playground, looking nervous. The other children are running and playing, but she is afraid to join them.

Close-up of her worried face.

---

2–4 seconds

Johra closes her eyes, raises her small hands, and softly whispers:

**"حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ"**

Transliteration:

**"Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel."**

English subtitle:

**"Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best disposer of affairs."**

---

4–7 seconds

A gentle breeze moves her hijab. Johra opens her eyes and smiles confidently.

Two children walk toward her and invite her to play.

Child:

"Come play with us!"

Johra smiles and runs toward them.

---

7–10 seconds

The camera follows Johra as she laughs and plays with her new friends.

On-screen text:

❤️ Trust Allah in every situation.

📖 Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel.

🎁 Learn Quran and duas with Riwaq Al Ilm.

CAMERA

- Start with a close-up of Johra's worried expression.
- Slow cinematic push-in as she recites the dua.
- Smooth transition to the children approaching her.
- End with a joyful wide shot of the playground.

AUDIO

- Soft child voice
- Gentle wind and birds
- Children's laughter in the distance
- Soft nasheed humming (no instruments)

IMPORTANT

The dua must be recited slowly and clearly:

**"Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel."**

Keep the emotions natural, comforting, and inspiring.

Riwaq Al Ilm
